## Detailed description
Turn Google Drive PDFs into instant slideshows with one click. Your PDFs. One click. Fullscreen.

YOUR FILES STAY YOURS: ZERO DATA SHARING
• Nothing is ever sent to us or to anyone else. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking, so there is nothing for us to collect.
• Your PDF is held only temporarily, in your own browser's memory, just long enough to show the slides. It is never saved to your disk, and it is gone the moment you close the slideshow.
• It is rendered inside the extension's own isolated page, which other websites cannot read.
• DriveSlide can only talk to Google Drive. That is enforced by the extension's built-in security policy, not just promised.
• DriveSlide never reads your password, cookies or sign-in tokens, and it never gets around Drive's sharing or download restrictions.
• No remote code. The PDF renderer (Mozilla pdf.js) is bundled inside the extension, so nothing can be swapped in after install.

THE PROBLEM
Drive previews a PDF as a long vertical stack of pages. To present it you normally have to download the file and open it in another app.

HOW DriveSlide WORKS
1. In Google Drive, select a PDF (or open its preview).
2. Click the "▶ Present PDF" button that appears, or right-click the file and choose "Present as slideshow".
3. The PDF opens as a fullscreen slideshow right in your Drive tab. Press Esc or ✕ to return.

You can also use the toolbar icon to present the PDF in the current tab, or paste any Drive link into it.

FEATURES
• One slide at a time, scaled to fit your screen
• Hover arrows, keyboard, click and a seek bar for navigation
• Optional auto-advance (1, 2, 5 or 10 seconds) with a progress line
• Slide overview grid for jumping anywhere
• Laser pointer, black screen and presentation timer
• Press ? for all shortcuts

GOOD TO KNOW
• Works with PDFs your Google account can already download. If an owner has disabled downloading for viewers, DriveSlide can't present the file and will say so.
• Google Docs, Slides and other non-PDF files are not supported.

## Install and use (for the support page)
1. Install DriveSlide from the Chrome Web Store.
2. Open drive.google.com and sign in (DriveSlide uses your existing session).
3. Select a PDF: the **▶ Present PDF** button appears at the bottom right. Click it, or right-click the file → **Present as slideshow**.
4. Navigate with ← → / Space / click, **↑ ↓** for auto-advance, **G** for the overview, **L** for the laser, **B** for a black screen, **F** for fullscreen and **?** for help.
5. From the toolbar icon you can also present the current Drive PDF tab or paste a Drive link.

### Suggested screenshots (all must show real, current behaviour)
1. **Entry point.** A Drive folder with one PDF selected and the **▶ Present PDF** button visible. Caption: "One click from Drive".
2. **Context menu.** Right-click on a PDF showing "Present as slideshow" (only if this works on current Drive markup).
3. **The slideshow.** A slide fullscreen in the Drive tab with the bottom control bar visible.
4. **Overview grid.** Press `G`. Caption: "Jump to any slide".
5. **Auto-advance and shortcuts.** The ⏱ menu open, or the `?` shortcuts panel.

Capture tips: use a sample PDF with no private content, hide personal info (names, email, other file names), use a 1280×800 browser window, and don't add mock features.

## Promo tile design direction (when you create it)
Plain dark background, the DriveSlide icon, the name "DriveSlide", the subtitle "PDF Slideshow for Google Drive", and the tagline "Your PDFs. One click. Fullscreen." No Google logos, no Drive iconography, no "official" or "for Google" badges.

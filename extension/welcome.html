DriveSlide

PDF Slideshow for Google Drive

Your PDFs.
One click.
Fullscreen.

Present a PDF from Drive as a focused, fullscreen slideshow. Your file is fetched from Google and rendered in your browser’s memory.

DriveSlide is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Get started

Present a PDF

  1. Select one PDFOpen Drive and select a PDF. The ▶ Present PDF button appears.
  2. Start presentingClick the button to open the slideshow over your Drive tab.
  3. Move through slidesUse ← / → or Space. Press ? to see all shortcuts.
⌨

Presenter controls

Auto-advance, overview grid, laser pointer, black screen, timer and fullscreen.

◈

Private by design

No account, server or analytics. The chosen PDF is not saved to disk by DriveSlide.

↗

Start from the toolbar

Use the DriveSlide icon to present the current Drive PDF or paste a Drive link.
